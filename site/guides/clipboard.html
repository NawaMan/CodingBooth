Copy & paste with a desktop booth

A desktop booth (XFCE, KDE, LXQt, Wayland) runs inside your browser over VNC, so your computer and the booth each have their own clipboard. noVNC's Clipboard panel connects them. This page shows how to use it, with a short video for each direction.

Why a panel?

Copy and paste work normally inside the desktop. To move text between the desktop and your computer, it goes through the panel.

The desktop runs inside the container. Your browser only receives a picture of it over VNC. When you press Ctrl+C in a booth app, the text goes to the booth's clipboard. When you copy on your computer, it goes to your clipboard. The two do not sync on their own.

noVNC, the web viewer that shows the desktop, has a Clipboard panel with a text box. Text you put in the box goes to the booth's clipboard. Text you copy inside the booth shows up in the box.

The same explanation is in the booth's CodingBooth Help dialog, under the Clipboard tab.

Open the Clipboard panel

Two clicks on the left edge of the desktop.

  1. Click the small tab on the left edge. It is a thin gray handle with an arrow, halfway down the desktop. The noVNC control bar slides out.
  2. Click the clipboard icon. It is the second button (hover over it to see the Clipboard tooltip). A panel titled Clipboard opens with a text box.
  3. Leave it open while you copy and paste. You can still use the desktop. Click the icon again to close the panel.

Tips

  • Plain text only. The panel carries text. Images, files and rich formatting do not go through it.
  • Inside the desktop, nothing changes. Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V between booth apps work like on any desktop. Use the panel only to cross between the booth and your computer.
  • If copied booth text does not show up in the box, copy it again in the booth app, using the app's own Copy (menu or Ctrl+C).
  • In a booth terminal, copy is usually Ctrl+Shift+C and paste is Ctrl+Shift+V, because plain Ctrl+C stops the running program.
  • The panel keeps only the last text. A new copy on either side replaces it.

Why the panel is a manual step: browsers only let a web page read your clipboard with your permission, and noVNC's automatic clipboard sync is not in a released version yet. When it is, booths will pick it up and this page will be updated.
