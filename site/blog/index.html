The CodingBooth Blog

Notes from inside the booth — short, focused deep dives into the bits and pieces that make CodingBooth tick.

CodingBooth delivers fully reproducible, isolated development environments in a Docker container that runs as you, with your project files staying yours on the host. This blog takes it apart one piece at a time — a feature, a design decision, or a workflow per post.

Each post comes in two shapes: click through it as a slide deck, or read it as a long-form page. New posts land here over time.
