\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

Table~\ref{tab:counts} lists $\pi(x)$ next to the estimate $x / \ln x$
\citep{hardy2008}.

\begin{table}[h]
  \centering
  \begin{tabular}{rrrr}
    \toprule
    $x$ & $\pi(x)$ & $x / \ln x$ & ratio \\
    \midrule
    $10^2$ & 25     & 21.7     & 1.151 \\
    $10^3$ & 168    & 144.8    & 1.161 \\
    $10^4$ & 1\,229 & 1\,085.7 & 1.132 \\
    $10^5$ & 9\,592 & 8\,685.9 & 1.104 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \caption{Exact prime counts against the prime number theorem.}
  \label{tab:counts}
\end{table}

The counts come from a sieve of Eratosthenes:

\begin{lstlisting}[language=Python]
def primes_up_to(n):
    sieve = [True] * (n + 1)
    sieve[0:2] = [False, False]
    for i in range(2, int(n ** 0.5) + 1):
        if sieve[i]:
            sieve[i*i::i] = [False] * len(sieve[i*i::i])
    return [i for i, p in enumerate(sieve) if p]
\end{lstlisting}
